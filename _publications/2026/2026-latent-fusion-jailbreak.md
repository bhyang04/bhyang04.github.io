abstract: >-
  Pairs harmful queries with structurally similar benign counterparts and uses refusal-loss gradients to choose layers and token positions for hidden-state interpolation. Across five open-weight models and four safety benchmarks, it reaches 94.01% average attack success; latent-space adversarial training reduces attack success by over 80%.
