**I am actively looking for a PhD position for Fall 2027.**

I am an undergraduate student pursuing a B.Sc. (Honors) in Computer Science and Technology at [Beijing Normal–Hong Kong Baptist University](https://www.bnbu.edu.cn/en/). I have worked as a research intern at [IFRC Lab](https://ifrc-zju.github.io/), Zhejiang University, with [Dr. Wenpeng Xing](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=2S80TvwAAAAJ) and [Prof. Meng Han](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=TnCrl1cAAAAJ).

My research interests include **large language models**, especially **trustworthy and efficient LLMs**, vision-language models, and **3D Gaussian Splatting**. Recent work includes [**TriLens**](https://tosakaucw.github.io/TriLens/) for white-box hallucination detection, [**DECENT**](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1665/) for VLM misclassification detection, and **TTS-Bench** for understanding answer discovery and candidate selectability in test-time scaling.

I also have a strong background in competitive programming, with experience in NOIP and ICPC/CCPC. I am a Codeforces Master with a peak rating of **2183** and received a **Bronze Medal** at the ICPC Asia-East Regional Contest in Hong Kong.

I welcome research discussions and collaborations. Please feel free to reach out by email.
